About self-talk from the scriptures

What are daily Bible affirmations?

For many years now athletes, entrepreneurs, entertainers and other success-oriented people have used the power of positive affirmations to re-program their minds for personal growth and achievement. However, Self-Talk from the Scriptures is the world's first audio affirmation program created directly from the Bible to help believers replace their old thought processes with truths from God's word.

"This program isn't just quoting scripture — it's scripture in the voice of proven, effective self-talk."

Our story & mission

The story

In her early twenties, Princess L. Jordan discovered the power of self-talk and saw how closely it lined up with what the Bible already says about the power of our words. Self-Talk from the Scriptures was born from that discovery.
